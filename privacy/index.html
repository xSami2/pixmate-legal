Pixmate Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Pixmate ("Pixmate: Trip Photos") lets people on the same trip share photos and videos directly between their phones. It is built so that we, the developer, never receive your photos, trips or personal information.

No account, no Pixmate server

Pixmate has no sign-up and asks for no email address or phone number. Your phone is identified by a random ID created when you install the app. There is no Pixmate server: trips, photos, videos, expenses and notes are stored on your device and sent only to the devices of the trip members you invite, directly over your local network, using encrypted connections.

What trip members see

People in a trip with you see the name and colour you choose, your device type, and the photos, videos, expenses and other items you add to that trip. Nearby devices that are not in your trip only see a random device ID.

Permissions

  • Photos and camera: to add photos and videos to a trip, take new ones, scan invitation QR codes, and save photos shared by trip members to your library.
  • Microphone: to record sound with videos taken in the trip camera.
  • Local network: to find trip members on the same Wi-Fi and exchange items with them.
  • Location (optional): to show where photos were taken and, when you tap Near me, which suggested places are closest. Your location stays on your device and is not tracked.
  • Health (optional, iPhone): Pixmate reads your daily step count during a trip only if you allow it, and shares it only with the trips you choose, so the group can see who walked the most. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never sent to us or to any third party.

Discover Places (iPhone)

To suggest places, Pixmate sends Apple Maps the trip's destination and the kind of place you are looking for. It never sends your photos, names or who is on the trip. Apple's handling of these requests is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

On-device AI

Features such as asking questions about a trip use Apple's on-device model. Your trip data is processed on your phone and is not sent to us.

Purchases

Pixmate Plus is billed by the App Store (or Google Play on Android). Purchases are verified by RevenueCat, Inc. under an anonymous ID that is not linked to your name or trips. RevenueCat receives purchase information (such as the product and transaction details) but never your photos or trip data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Pixmate contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Everything Pixmate stores is on your device. Deleting a trip removes it from your phone; deleting the app removes all of Pixmate's data from your phone. Copies already shared with other trip members stay on their devices, under their control.

Children

Pixmate is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
