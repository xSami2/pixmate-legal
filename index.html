Pixmate

Trip photos, shared directly between your group's phones.

Support

Need help or found a problem? Open an issue at github.com/xSami2/pixmate-legal/issues.

Common questions

Photos aren't arriving. Make sure everyone in the trip has Pixmate open on the same Wi-Fi network and has allowed Local Network access.

How do I cancel Pixmate Plus? In iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pixmate.

Where is my data? Only on your phone and your trip members' phones. There is no Pixmate server.
